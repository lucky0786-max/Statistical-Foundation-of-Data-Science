*** Aim***
To analyse the TeachingRatings dataset and study the relationship between professors' age, gender, tenure, course division and teaching evaluation scores using Python.

***What I Did***
1. Loaded the TeachingRatings dataset using Pandas.
2. Identified duplicate professor records and calculated the mean and standard deviation of age before and after removing duplicates.
3. Used a bar chart to compare average teaching evaluations of lower and upper-division courses.
4. Created a scatter plot to study the relationship between age and teaching evaluation.
5. Compared age and teaching evaluation based on gender.
6. Created a scatter plot showing the relationship between age, evaluation, gender and tenure.

***Conclusion***
In this practical, I performed data cleaning and exploratory data analysis using Pandas, Matplotlib and Seaborn. I found that lower-division courses had slightly higher average evaluations than upper-division courses. The scatter plots showed no strong relationship between professors' age and teaching evaluation scores.
